About Ruthless

Ruthless was created in 2025 but it first started as Athena and retained the name for at least 2 months. The leaders thought of something new. The former Acting guild leader - Rex, brought the name Ruthless and they all agreed to it and have been pushing the guild since then. Ruthless isn't just a guild. It's a squad built around skill, loyalty, teamwork and hunger to improve. Every member represents the name.

Our Mission

  • Build a strong competitive team
  • Improve members' skills
  • Participate in tournaments
  • Become a loyal community
  • Become recognized in the Free Fire community

Guild Rules

  • At least 2k points from each member
  • Respect other members
  • Respect the leaders
  • No unnecessary toxicity
  • Help teammates improve

Recruitment

  • Minimum level: Level 40
  • Required Rank: Platinum in CS or BR
  • No age limit
