01 / WHO WE ARE

OUR STORY

NOT JUST A NAME.
A STANDARD.

Ruthless was created in 2025, but the journey began under a different name: Athena.

The guild kept that name for at least two months. Then its leaders decided it was time for something new. Guild Leader Ragna and former Acting Guild Leader Rex came up with the name Ruthless, and the members agreed to move forward together.

Today, Ruthless stands for a squad built around skill, loyalty, teamwork and the hunger to improve. Every member represents the name.
